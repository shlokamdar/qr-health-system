# Chapter 8: User Manual

Welcome to the **Pulse Health QR System**. This guide provides comprehensive, step-by-step instructions for all users of the platform.

---

## 1. PATIENT USER MANUAL
*Manage your digital health identity and clinical records.*

### A. Getting Started
1.  **Registration:** Visit the 'Register' page, select **Patient**, and fill in your details (Name, DOB, Email, etc.).
2.  **Health ID Card:** Once logged in, your **Digital Health ID (HID)** is displayed. 
    - Click **View ID Card** to see your unique QR code.
    - Click **Download QR** to save it for offline use during clinic visits.
3.  **Profile Management:** Use the **Edit Profile** button to update your blood group, contact number, or address at any time.

### B. Giving Access to Doctors
1.  **QR Scanning:** When visiting a clinic, present your QR code. The doctor will scan it to identify you.
2.  **Approve Access (Dashboard):** If a doctor requests access, a notification will appear on your dashboard. Click **Approve** to grant them entry to your medical timeline.
3.  **OTP Authorization:** For more secure or remote access, a doctor may request an **OTP**. 
    - Find the **6-digit code** on your dashboard under "Live Requests".
    - Provide this code to the doctor to unlock your full medical history.

### C. Managing Health Records
1.  **Medical History:** View all past consultations, diagnoses, and prescriptions in the **Medical Records** tab.
2.  **Lab Reports:** Access digital versions of diagnostic tests (e.g., blood tests, imaging) in the **Lab Reports** section.
3.  **Digital Vault:** Use **Upload Document** to securely store your old physical prescriptions or external reports.
4.  **Download PDF:** Click **Full Medical History** to generate a password-protected PDF summary of your entire record.

### D. Emergency & Appointments
1.  **Emergency Contacts:** Add trusted family members or friends. These contacts can be authorized to provide emergency-level access to your records if you are incapacitated.
2.  **Book Appointment:** Search for a doctor and use the **Book Appointment** feature to schedule a visit directly through the portal.

### E. Privacy Control
1.  **Sharing Permissions:** View a live list of all doctors and hospitals who currently have access to your data.
2.  **Immediate Revocation:** Click **Revoke Access** next to any entry to instantly terminate their access to your medical history.
3.  **Access Logs:** Visit the **Audit History** tab to see exactly who accessed your profile and at what time.

## 2. DOCTOR USER MANUAL
*Access patient history and manage your clinical practice.*

### A. Identifying Patients
1.  **Scan QR:** Use the **Scan QR** button on your dashboard and point your camera at the patient's Health ID.
2.  **Manual Search:** If the QR is unavailable, enter the patient's **Health ID (HID)** into the search bar.
3.  **Registration:** If a patient is new to the system, you can register them directly using the **Register New Patient** form in your portal.

### B. Clinical Workflow
1.  **Request Full History:** If you do not have permission, click **Request OTP** to prompt the patient for authorization.
2.  **View Records:** Once authorized, you can view the patient's full medical history, including past consultations, lab reports, and allergies.
3.  **Record Consultation:** Create a new entry for the session. Input Chief Complaint, Symptoms, Diagnosis, and Prescribed Medicines.
4.  **Upload Findings:** Attach diagnostic images or notes directly to the consultation record.

### C. Practice Management
1.  **Appointment Queue:** View and manage your daily bookings in the **My Appointments** tab.
2.  **Dashboard Stats:** Monitor your practice growth through consultation counts and patient visit analytics.

---

## 3. HOSPITAL ADMIN MANUAL
*Administer hospital departments, staff, and facility operations.*

### A. Institutional Management
1.  **Department Setup:** Create organizational units (e.g., "Radiology", "Pediatrics") and manage their descriptions.
2.  **Doctor Roster:** View all doctors affiliated with your facility. Assign them to specific departments and track their verification status.
3.  **Staff Onboarding:** Create secure accounts for **Lab Technicians** directly from your dashboard.

### B. Auditing & Monitoring
1.  **Visitation Logs:** Review a complete, filterable table of all consultations performed within the hospital.
2.  **Facility Stats:** Monitor hospital-wide performance metrics, including total doctor count, pending verifications, and total patient visits.
3.  **Profile Configuration:** Update official hospital contact information, registration numbers, and facility addresses.

---

## 4. LAB TECHNICIAN MANUAL
*Process diagnostic tests and upload reports.*

### A. Report Processing
1.  **Find Patient:** Enter the patient's **Health ID** to verify their identity.
2.  **Upload Report:** Select the **Test Category** (CBC, Lipid Profile, etc.), input key findings, and upload the report file (PDF/Image).
3.  **Verification:** Once uploaded, the report is instantly available to the student (and authorized doctors).

### B. Tracking & History
1.  **Upload History:** View an audit trail of all reports you have uploaded to the system.
2.  **Patient Lookup:** View a patient's entire diagnostic repository by searching their Health ID to understand their medical context.

---

## 5. SYSTEM ADMIN (MASTER) MANUAL
*System-wide governance, security, and support.*

### A. Verification Workflow
1.  **Pending Applications:** Review new registration requests from Doctors, Hospitals, and Labs.
2.  **Accreditation Review:** Inspect digital copies of medical licenses and registration certificates.
3.  **Approval/Rejection:** Approve genuine entities to unlock their dashboards. Provide a formal **Rejection Reason** for applications that do not meet requirements.

### B. Operations & Support
1.  **Support Tickets:** Monitor and resolve user-reported issues. Provide resolution notes for every ticket.
2.  **User Management:** Globally search, edit, or terminate any user account (Patient, Doctor, etc.) across the platform.

### C. Security Oversight
1.  **Universal Access Logs:** Monitor every sensitive action across the system to ensure privacy compliance.
2.  **System Analytics:** Access high-level charts showing system growth, user distribution, and security trends.

---

## FAQ & TROUBLESHOOTING

- **QR Scan Fails?** Ensure camera permissions are granted. If on mobile, try wiping the lens and ensuring there is no glare.
- **OTP Not appearing?** OTPs are session-specific and appear on the **Patient's Dashboard**. The patient must be logged in.
- **Access Denied?** Verification is required for Doctors and Institutions. If your account is "Pending," please contact the System Admin.
- **How is data protected?** All medical history transfers are encrypted. Patients have final authority to grant or revoke access at any time.
